\subsection{Validation-locked architecture selection}
\label{sec:factorial_results}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------

The complete paired factorial is reported in Table~\ref{tab:factorial} and
Fig.~\ref{fig:factorial}.  Validation MAE selected variant
\PRMSelectedVariant, with a mean of
\PRMSelectedValidationMAE\eV{} (95\% bootstrap interval
[\PRMSelectedValidationMAELow, \PRMSelectedValidationMAEHigh]\eV{}) across
the five paired repeats.  Its locked test MAE was
\PRMSelectedTestMAE\eV{} [\PRMSelectedTestMAELow,
\PRMSelectedTestMAEHigh]\eV{}.  The test values were exposed only after the
selection rule had fixed the promoted architecture.

\input{generated/tab_factorial.tex}

\begin{figure*}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{fig_factorial.pdf}
\caption{Paired architecture experiment.
  (a) Validation and locked-test MAE for all eight G/E/P variants; shading
  marks the validation-selected variant.
  (b) Orthogonal main effects and interactions on validation and locked-test
  MAE, with 95\% bootstrap intervals over paired repeats.  Negative effects
  reduce MAE.}
\label{fig:factorial}
\end{figure*}

The orthogonal contrasts separate evidence for the three prespecified modules
from variation between random partitions.  Because P jointly changes
normalization placement and distance gating, its contrast supports or rejects
that composite block only; it does not identify either subchange as the causal
source of an effect.

\subsection{Interpolation, chemical transfer, and low-energy behavior}
\label{sec:transfer_results}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------

Using the promoted architecture, pooled DART test MAEs were
\PRMIdCvDARTMAE\eV{} for random out-of-fold prediction,
\PRMPairCvDARTMAE\eV{} when host--impurity pairs were held out,
\PRMHostCvDARTMAE\eV{} for held-out hosts,
\PRMDopantCvDARTMAE\eV{} for held-out impurities, and
\PRMChemistryBlockDARTMAE\eV{} for the predefined chemistry block.
Table~\ref{tab:benchmark} compares the aligned DART, periodic SchNet, and
validation-selected descriptor predictions.  The paired intervals, rather
than unpaired differences between aggregate scores, determine whether a
directional model difference is supported.

  (b) Paired differences in absolute error relative to DART with 95\%
  bootstrap intervals using the regime-matched resampling units defined in

Aggregate MAE can hide failures in chemically sparse or screening-relevant
subsets.  Table~\ref{tab:applicability} therefore reports equal-weight host and
impurity macro errors together with performance on nonpositive formation
energies and the lowest-energy decile.  For example, on pair-held-out
predictions, the true low-decile MAE was
\PRMPairCvDARTLowEnergyMAE\eV{} and its recall by the predicted low decile was
\PRMPairCvDARTLowEnergyRecall\%.  None of these low-energy summaries entered
architecture or hyperparameter selection.

\subsection{Held-out uncertainty and selective prediction}
\label{sec:uq_results}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------

On the untouched uncertainty test partition, the five-member ensemble had
MAE \PRMUQTestMAE\eV{}, Gaussian negative log likelihood \PRMUQNLL, and mean
Gaussian CRPS \PRMUQCRPS\eV{}.  The Spearman correlation between calibrated
standard deviation and absolute error was \PRMUQErrorSpearman.  The
risk--coverage curve had AURC \PRMUQAURC\eV{} and excess AURC
\PRMUQExcessAURC\eV{} relative to oracle error ordering.

\input{generated/tab_uq.tex}

At 90\% nominal coverage, the split-conformal interval covered
\PRMUQNinetyCoverage\% of test structures with mean width
\PRMUQNinetyWidth\eV{}.  Table~\ref{tab:uq} reports all prespecified nominal

\subsection{Out-of-fold screening decisions within IMP2D}
\label{sec:screening_results}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------

The host--impurity-pair folds provide one out-of-fold prediction for every
retained structure.  Across pairs containing both incorporation classes, DART
recovered the lower-energy class with
\PRMPreferenceAccuracy\% accuracy; the predicted and reference class margins
had Spearman correlation \PRMPreferenceMarginSpearman and margin MAE
\PRMPreferenceMarginMAE\eV{}.  Selecting one configuration globally gave
\PRMGlobalExactAccuracy\% exact-site accuracy and mean regret
\PRMGlobalRegret\eV{}.  When the incorporation class was fixed, exact and
top-two site accuracies were \PRMWithinTypeExactAccuracy\% and
\PRMWithinTypeTopTwoAccuracy\%, respectively, with mean regret
\PRMWithinTypeRegret\eV{}.
