\title{Defect-aware graph transformer for impurity formation energies in
two-dimensional materials: Chemical transferability and error analysis}

\author{Yiming Hua}
\affiliation{Wuhan University, Wuhan, China}
% Author-controlled submission blocker: insert the verified full author list,

\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
\ifprmresultsready
We develop the Defect-Aware Radial Transformer (DART), an impurity-centered
graph model with a strictly permutation-invariant local representation and
exact minimum-image distances, and evaluate the chemical transferability of
its impurity formation-energy predictions on 10,224 neutral structures
curated from the IMP2D database.  The task is retrospective estimation and
reranking over available DFT-relaxed candidate geometries.  Because each geometry and
its reference energy are products of the same completed DFT workflow, the task
evaluates database-conditioned prediction rather than replacing candidate
generation or relaxation.  Transfer is organized by chemical novelty in a
strict hierarchy: mean absolute error grows only mildly from random
cross-validation (\PRMRepIdCvMAE\eV{}) through withheld host--impurity pairs
(\PRMRepPairCvMAE\eV{}) and a predefined chemistry block
(\PRMRepChemBlockMAE\eV{}), but roughly doubles when a complete impurity
(\PRMRepDopantCvMAE\eV{}) or host (\PRMRepHostCvMAE\eV{}) is withheld.  The
small but significant host--impurity asymmetry tracks the structural novelty
of the held-out host after controlling for training-support asymmetry.
Incorporation geometry forms a second axis with distinct physics: interstitial
structures (\PRMRepPairInterstitialMAE\eV{}) are over twice as hard as
adsorbates (\PRMRepPairAdsorbateMAE\eV{}), and preregistered error maps show
interstitial error concentrating in roomy, loosely constrained cages ---
large clearance and neighbor distances with uniform spacing --- and rising
with valence mismatch, whereas adsorbate error tracks chemical mismatch,
led by signed valence.  A readout analysis connects model design to
the non-extensive, defect-local character of the formation energy: an
additive-readout baseline degrades to \PRMSchNetAddHostCvMAE\eV{} on withheld
hosts and improves to \PRMSchNetMeanHostCvMAE\eV{} with a mean readout alone,
while the defect-centered model shows no size-dependent residual drift.  For
candidates sharing a host and impurity, out-of-fold predictions select the
lower-energy incorporation class with \PRMRepPreferenceAccuracy\% accuracy
against a \PRMRepPreferenceReference\% class-prior reference, with
\PRMRepWithinRegret\eV{} mean within-class site-selection regret.  All
reported numbers come from the repaired representation; a permutation
diagnostic of the superseded implementation shifted single predictions by up
to several eV while pooled errors moved by under $10^{-3}$\,eV, showing that
aggregate benchmarks can hide representation defects that dominate individual
predictions.
\else
We study how impurity formation-energy prediction changes across host,
impurity, and incorporation-geometry axes in two-dimensional materials.  The
analysis uses a defect-aware graph Transformer and a curated subset
of IMP2D; numerical results are available only when the corresponding audited
result bundle is present.
\fi
\end{abstract}
