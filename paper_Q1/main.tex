\title{Axis-resolved transfer of impurity formation energies in
two-dimensional materials with a defect-aware graph transformer}

\author{Yiming Hua}
\affiliation{Wuhan University, Wuhan, China}
% TODO: add other authors

\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
\ifprmresultsready
Machine-learned formation energies can accelerate impurity screening in
two-dimensional materials, but random-split accuracy does not show how a model
behaves when chemical identities are absent from target supervision.  We
develop the Defect-Aware Radial Transformer (DART) and evaluate it on 10,224
provenance-audited neutral IMP2D structures.  In a paired $2^3$ factorial,
gated readout and the composite pre-normalized, distance-gated local block
consistently reduced validation mean absolute error (MAE), whereas the
independent main effect of explicit environment enrichment was inconclusive; a
fixed validation-only rule selected the all-enabled variant.  Pair-held-out MAE
(\PRMPairCvDARTMAE\eV{}) remained close to random out-of-fold MAE
(\PRMIdCvDARTMAE\eV{}), whereas withholding complete hosts or impurities
increased MAE to \PRMHostCvDARTMAE{} and \PRMDopantCvDARTMAE\eV{},
respectively.  The selected DART learning recipe had lower paired absolute
error than the prespecified additive-readout periodic SchNet recipe and
split-local validation-selected descriptor baselines in all five evaluation
regimes.  Pair-held-out predictions retrospectively identified the lower-energy
incorporation class among retained IMP2D candidates with
\PRMPreferenceAccuracy\% accuracy and incurred \PRMGlobalRegret\eV{} mean
global site-selection regret.  At a nominal 90\% level, conformal intervals on
a separate internal ensemble split yielded \PRMUQNinetyCoverage\% empirical
marginal coverage at \PRMUQNinetyWidth\eV{} mean width.  These results define a
bounded screening domain within IMP2D; they do not establish prospective
performance outside the audited database.
\else
Machine-learned formation energies can accelerate impurity screening in
two-dimensional materials, but random-split accuracy alone does not establish
axis-resolved transfer or a reliable deployment domain.  We define a
provenance-audited, leakage-controlled evaluation of a defect-aware graph
transformer on IMP2D.  Numerical claims are withheld until all prespecified
non-DFT analyses and paper assets pass their provenance checks.
\fi
\end{abstract}

\section*{Data and code availability}
The source IMP2D data are available as version 2 of the Interstitial and
Adsorbate Structure Database~\cite{imp2d_dataset2022}; their construction is
described in Ref.~\cite{imp2d2023}.  The exact sample audit, immutable split
definitions, resolved experiment configurations, analysis scripts, and
machine-readable result bundles for this study are available at
the current review-stage branch,
\url{https://github.com/chimeraHHH/2d-defect-dast/tree/prm-revision}.  A
release-specific commit, formal software/data citation, and archival identifier
will replace this provisional branch link before submission.
