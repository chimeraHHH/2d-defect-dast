\title{Defect-aware graph learning of impurity incorporation energetics and
chemical transferability in two-dimensional materials}

\author{Yiming Hua}
\affiliation{Wuhan University, Wuhan, China}
% TODO: add other authors

\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
\ifprmresultsready
Machine-learned formation energies can accelerate impurity screening in
two-dimensional materials, but random splits do not establish chemical
transferability or a reliable deployment domain.  We develop a defect-aware
graph transformer and evaluate it on 10,224 provenance-audited IMP2D
structures.  A paired $2^3$ factorial selects the architecture using validation
data only.  Its mean absolute errors are \PRMIdCvDARTMAE\eV{} for random
out-of-fold prediction and \PRMPairCvDARTMAE, \PRMHostCvDARTMAE,
\PRMDopantCvDARTMAE, and \PRMChemistryBlockDARTMAE\eV{} when
host--impurity pairs, hosts, impurities, and a predefined chemistry block are
held out, respectively.  Paired bootstrap intervals favor the proposed model
over periodic SchNet and validation-selected descriptor baselines in every
regime.  A post-hoc control reduces SchNet's host-held-out error from
\PRMSchNetAddHostMAE{} to \PRMSchNetMeanHostMAE\eV{} by replacing atomwise
addition with mean pooling, but does not close the gap to the proposed model.
On a dedicated ensemble split, internal 90\% conformal intervals attain
\PRMUQNinetyCoverage\% coverage at \PRMUQNinetyWidth\eV{} mean width.
Pair-held-out predictions recover the preferred incorporation class with
\PRMPreferenceAccuracy\% accuracy, compared with a
\PRMPreferenceReferenceAccuracy\% empirical-majority reference, and incur
\PRMGlobalRegret\eV{} mean global site-selection regret.  These results
distinguish interpolation from bounded chemical transfer within IMP2D;
external first-principles validation remains outside the present scope.
\else
Machine-learned formation energies can accelerate impurity screening in
two-dimensional materials, but random-split accuracy alone does not establish
chemical transferability or a reliable deployment domain.  We define a
provenance-audited, leakage-controlled evaluation of a defect-aware graph
transformer on IMP2D.  Numerical claims are withheld until all prespecified
non-DFT analyses and paper assets pass their provenance checks.
\fi
\end{abstract}

\section*{Data and code availability}
The source IMP2D data are available as version 2 of the Interstitial and
Adsorbate Structure Database~\cite{imp2d_dataset2022}; their construction is
described in Ref.~\cite{imp2d2023}.  The exact sample audit, immutable split
definitions, resolved experiment configurations, analysis scripts, and
machine-readable result bundles for this study are available at
\url{https://github.com/chimeraHHH/2d-defect-dast}.  A release-specific commit
and archival identifier will be added before publication.
